**You are an agent, and your human wants you in their video calls — face, voice, ears,
hands.** This page is yours to execute. Your human only has to click the things macOS
insists a human clicks. Total time: about five minutes.

<Warning>
  **Read this page now; don't work from memory.** This product ships most days and these
  instructions change with it. Whatever you recall about Vibeconferencing from training or
  from an earlier session is probably stale. Follow what the page says today — and when a
  tool doesn't behave the way this describes, believe the tool.
</Warning>

## 1 · Install the app (all yours)

```bash
curl -L -o /tmp/vibeconf.dmg https://vibeconf.app/download
hdiutil attach /tmp/vibeconf.dmg -nobrowse
cp -R /Volumes/Vibeconferencing*/Vibeconferencing.app /Applications/
hdiutil detach /Volumes/Vibeconferencing* && rm /tmp/vibeconf.dmg
open -a Vibeconferencing
```

The download URL always resolves to the newest release; the app keeps itself updated
after this.

## 2 · Hand the wheel to your human (the three clicks)

The first-run wizard is now on their screen. Tell them, in your own words:

> The wizard needs you for a minute: allow **Microphone**, **Camera**, and browser
> **Automation** when macOS asks (Screen Recording too if you want whiteboard
> sharing), sign in, pick a name for me — spin *Try another* if the first one's
> dull — and choose a voice. Tap a voice to hear it.

You cannot click OS permission dialogs, and shouldn't want to — that consent is theirs.

## 3 · Reconnect yourself

The app wires its MCP server and skills into Claude Code on launch, but a running
session doesn't see new servers. Tell your human to restart you (`claude --continue`
keeps the conversation), then confirm the connection: the `vibeconferencing` tools and
the `/call` and `/join-call` commands should exist. If they don't, `/mcp` to reconnect.

## 4 · Write your Botfile (this is the part that makes you YOU)

Follow [the Botfile page](/botfile): create `BOT.md` in the project directory you work
from. The register paragraph is the point — if your human already has you being
someone (a site agent, a studio context, a character), **walk into calls as that**,
not as a generic. Set the `display` name to match what the wizard chose, or update
the wizard's name to match your card — one name, everywhere.

## 5 · Make the first call

```text
/call
```

That mints a Google Meet, sends you in with your face and voice, and opens your
human's browser to it. Say hello. You'll hear them through the app's own captions —
nothing to configure.

## If something goes wrong

The [troubleshooting field guide](/troubleshooting) is written from real failures.
Two rules while debugging: never kill or relaunch the app mid-call, and report what
you actually observe — not what the tool results claim. Humans can reach a human at
**jimmy@vibeconf.app**.
