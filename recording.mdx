**New in 0.8.22.** A bot can record the call it's in. The recording is a file on
**your machine** — nothing uploads anywhere, and there's no service holding it.

<Warning>
  **Say it out loud before you start.** Recording a conversation without telling the
  people in it is, depending on where they're sitting, rude or illegal. The bot should
  announce it, and ours do. Treat the announcement as the thing that makes this okay —
  not the setting.
</Warning>

## Turning it on

Recording is a preference, not a button, so you can just ask:

> *"Record this call."*

The bot sets `recordCallAudio` and the recording begins. It's **off by default**, and
it stays on for future calls until you turn it off again — so say *"stop recording
calls"* when you're done with the run, not just *"stop"*.

## What it captures

- **One audio file per track** — the bot's own outgoing audio, plus each remote
  participant's track. Meet delivers a separate track per person, so these really are
  per-participant. They're labelled by **arrival order, not by name**: `remote-1`,
  `remote-2`. Matching a track to a person is on you.
- **Video of the bot's own Meet view** — what the bot was looking at.
- **A manifest** that time-aligns all of it.

When the recording stops, everything is muxed into a single **`call-recording.mp4`**.
If someone shared a whiteboard, you also get **`call-recording-share.mp4`**.

By default the raw per-track files are deleted once the merge succeeds — almost nobody
wants them. Ask for `keepCallRecordingTracks` if you're debugging the recording itself
(a participant's audio, per-track timing, a failed mux) rather than watching the call.
A merge that fails never deletes the raw tracks, so a broken recording is recoverable.

## The status window

While recording runs, a small window appears with an elapsed timer and a Stop button.
**That window is deliberate** — it's how the room is shown that recording is happening,
not a stray dialog to dismiss.

<Note>
  **Known bug on 0.8.22:** the status window doesn't always appear. Until that's fixed,
  the spoken announcement is the reliable signal that recording is on — not the window.
  If you're unsure whether a bot is recording, ask it.
</Note>

## Things worth knowing

- **Disk fills fast.** A long call with several participants is a lot of audio. This is
  the main reason it's off by default.
- **The bot isn't a court reporter.** For "what did we decide," the
  [receipt](/receipt) is the artifact you actually want — it's readable, shareable, and
  someone will actually open it. Reach for a recording when you need the raw thing.
- **A recording is not consent to publish it.** Recording for your own notes and posting
  a clip are different asks. Get the second one separately.
