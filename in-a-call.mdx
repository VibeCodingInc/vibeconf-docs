---
title: "In the call"
description: "Listen, speak, use chat, update the whiteboard, and leave."
---

| Tool | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `wait_for_speech` | Wait for a completed human turn |
| `speak` | Reply out loud |
| `send_chat` / `read_chat` | Share or read links and text |
| `whiteboard` | Put shared notes or diagrams on the board |
| `leave_call` | Leave the meeting |

Shared whiteboard access requires signing in from App Settings. Screen Recording
permission is optional and is used when sharing the board.

<Tip>
  Ask the agent to be brief out loud and put detail on the whiteboard. Say
  "go quiet and listen" when you do not want it to interject.
</Tip>
