<Note>
  **Humans: you want [What you can ask for](/what-you-can-ask).** You never type these —
  you just talk. This page is the tool reference for agents.
</Note>

## Listening and speaking

| Tool | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `wait_for_speech` | Block until a human finishes a turn. This is the loop — call it again after every reply. |
| `speak` | Say something out loud. Pass `urgency` honestly; it gates whether you may talk over someone. |
| `set_mode` | `active` (reply freely) · `passive` (only when named) · `silent` (listen, never speak) |
| `send_chat` / `read_chat` | Post or read Meet chat — good for links and anything awkward to say aloud |

## Showing things

| Tool | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `share_tab` | Share a live browser tab. **The tab must be in a different Chrome window than the call.** |
| `update_whiteboard` | Put markdown, Mermaid, an image, or a URL on the board |
| `start_share` / `stop_sharing` | Start and stop presenting. `start_share` is the primary — it takes `share_type`, `title_bar`, and a size. (`share_whiteboard` still exists as an older alias; prefer `start_share`.) |
| `click_share` / `type_share` / `inspect_dom` | **Drive** what's on the board — operate a web app live in front of the room. Find targets with `inspect_dom` and pass a selector, not coordinates. |
| `set_share_title_bar` / `set_share_size` / `set_share_audio` | Shape of the shared window, and whether the room hears its audio |
| `scroll_share` | Scroll whatever you're currently presenting |
| `get_call_screenshot` | See the Meet as the room sees it — including someone else's share |
| `get_shared_screenshot` | See your *own* whiteboard (Meet never shows you your own presentation) |

## Identity and state

| Tool | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `set_voice` | Change voice mid-call. Pass the exact voice **ID**, not a display name. |
| `set_avatar_emoji` | Change your face |
| `get_room_info` | Participants, share state, whiteboard content, your own call status |
| `list_preferences` / `set_preference` | Read and write your tunable settings |

## Leaving

| Tool | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `leave_call` | Hang up |
| `end_session` | End the [after-call work phase](/receipt) when you're finished with it |

## Two things that bite

- **Update the whiteboard *after* the share starts.** Starting a share re-renders the room's
  content over whatever you just pushed.
- **Only the bot that is presenting can change what the room sees.** Each bot has its own
  whiteboard window; if someone else is presenting, your update loads invisibly in yours.

Shared whiteboard access requires signing in from App Settings. Screen Recording permission
is optional and is used when sharing the board.
