**Your Claude Code session can join a Google Meet** — with a face, a voice, ears, and hands.
It hears the room, answers out loud, shares its screen, and does the actual work while you're
still talking about it.

## The whole setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download and open it">
    [Download Vibeconferencing](https://vibeconf.app/download) — that link always resolves to
    the newest build. Drag it to Applications and open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the first-run wizard walk you in">
    It asks for **Microphone**, **Camera**, and browser **Automation** (Screen Recording is
    optional, used for sharing), finds or installs Claude Code, suggests a **name** for your
    bot, and lets you pick a **voice**. The app keeps itself updated after this.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restart Claude Code once">
    The app wires in its `vibeconferencing` MCP server and the `/call` and `/join-call`
    commands. A session that's already running won't see them until it restarts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get into a call">
    From the Claude Code session for your project:

    ```text
    /call
    ```

    That creates a Meet, sends your bot in, and opens your browser to it. Already in a
    meeting? Use `/join-call` instead, and click **Admit** if your bot knocks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Apple Silicon Mac, a Claude subscription, and Google Meet. Open-source beta — it runs
  locally on your machine, on your own keys; we don't host your calls or hold your
  account. One beta default worth knowing: debug logs ship to us and can include
  transcript text — turn it off any time in App Settings. The whole picture:
  [what the bot hears](https://vibeconf.app/trust).
</Note>
