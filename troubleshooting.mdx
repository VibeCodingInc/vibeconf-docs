Every entry here comes from a real call. The pattern in almost all of them:
**the product is working, but it looks broken.** Read the "what it means"
line before reaching for a restart.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title='"Unable to connect to the API" in Claude Code' icon="plug-circle-xmark">
    **What it means:** the app process is gone, or it restarted on a
    different local port — the app walks from 7865 upward when its usual
    port is taken. It is **not** your Claude account, credits, or login.

    **Fix:** check the app is running. Hover the bot's name at the top of
    the app panel — the tooltip shows the live port (e.g. `local server
    port 7865`). Tell your session: *"use port 7865"*. Do **not** let the
    agent kill and relaunch the app — that is how this loop starts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title='"Potential risks" flag — and no Admit button' icon="shield-halved">
    **What it means:** Google Meet's guest queue (2026) flags unfamiliar
    joiners, and the **Admit** button is hidden behind the **⋮ overflow
    menu** on the flagged entry. It looks deny-only; it isn't.

    **Fix:** click **⋮** on the waiting entry to reveal **Admit**. If Meet
    keeps flagging the bot, it usually means the bot's browser profile is
    signed out or on an account with no history with the host — signing the
    bot's view into a known Google account restores the normal prompt.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title='Stuck at "Waiting to be admitted"' icon="hourglass">
    **What it means:** usually nothing is wrong — the host hasn't noticed
    the knock (or can't find Admit; see above). The bot waits indefinitely
    while Meet shows the waiting screen. Minutes-long waits are normal.

    **Fix:** host clicks **Admit** (check the ⋮ menu). If the host denied
    the request, the bot reports it — leave and run `/join-call` again.
    Resist restarting the app while it is knocking.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The agent joined but cannot hear anyone" icon="ear-deaf">
    **What it means:** the bot listens through Meet's captions. If captions
    are off, the bot is deaf — present, polite, and hearing nothing.

    **Fix:** the app turns captions on automatically at join; if speech
    stops coming through, check the captions toggle in the bot's Meet view.
    Also confirm Microphone and Camera permission in App Settings, then
    leave and `/join-call` again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The agent replies, but slightly off-topic every time" icon="language">
    **What it means:** the caption language doesn't match the language being
    spoken. Meet turns good speech into phonetic nonsense, and the agent
    answers the nonsense — confidently, and without any sign that it misheard.

    **Fix:** set the [call language](/caption-language). Read a few transcript
    lines to confirm — mangled captions are obvious once you look.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Two voices, one bot — it answers itself" icon="people-arrows">
    **What it means:** two agent sessions are driving the same bot. The
    app's **Join call** button spawns its own session — if you also ran
    `/join-call` from a terminal session, both are connected.

    **Fix:** one driver per bot. Close the app-spawned terminal (or have
    your session stand down). Pick the session that has your project's
    context — that's the one worth keeping.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="/join-call is missing" icon="plug">
    Make sure Vibeconferencing is open. Restart Claude Code once after installing
    the app. If the command is still missing, run `/mcp` and reconnect
    `vibeconferencing`.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The app cannot find the Meet" icon="video">
    Open the Meet in your browser and run `/join-call` again — or skip detection
    entirely: **copy the Meet URL, paste it into your Claude Code session, and say
    "join that call."** That path works even when auto-detection doesn't (it's how
    a beta user solved this in the field), and `/join-call <url>` accepts the full
    URL too.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The wrong bot joined — you have more than one" icon="shuffle">
    **What it means:** with several bot profiles installed, a bare `/join-call`
    drives the **default** profile — which today is the *first one you created*,
    not the one you used most recently. If Jessica was on your last call but
    Taylor shows up, this is why. (And if a bot "promised" it would join your next
    call — it can't arrange that on its own; the promise doesn't survive the call.)

    **Fix:** name the bot you want: `/join-call <meet-code> Jessica` — the name
    selects the profile. `list_call_instances` shows which profiles are running
    and targetable.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The bot won't stop presenting" icon="rectangle-xmark">
    **What it means:** occasionally the stop-share command reports sent but Meet
    keeps showing the bot as presenting — the share is wedged app-side. Seen in
    the field two days running; a fix is in the queue.

    **Fix:** anyone in the call can end it from Meet's side — **⋮ on the bot's
    tile → "Remove presentation."** The bot stays in the call and keeps working;
    only the stuck share is dropped. Meanwhile the bot can keep updating the
    board content, so nothing is lost.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The bot joined under the wrong name" icon="id-badge">
    **What it means:** the name is fixed at the moment the bot joins the
    Meet — it cannot change mid-call, no matter what the session believes.

    **Fix:** have the bot leave and rejoin under the right name. Check the
    profile's bot name in the app panel *before* joining.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The bot's voice keeps changing mid-call" icon="masks-theater">
    **What it means:** the bot has two voice paths — full replies carry the
    voice the agent asked for, but the little "mm-hmm" acknowledgments use a
    *saved default*. In the current beta the saved default doesn't survive an
    app restart, so the acks fall back to a system voice while the replies
    sound right. One bot, two voice boxes.

    **Fix:** at the start of a call, ask your agent to set the voice again
    ("use the Laura voice"). It takes effect immediately for that session.
    The persistence bug is tracked and being fixed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The bot went quiet — or backs off when someone coughs" icon="volume-slash">
    **What it means:** the bot yields the floor when it detects someone
    speaking. An experimental fast detector listens to raw audio energy, and
    on some setups a fan or keyboard reads as "someone is talking" — so the
    bot politely waits forever. It looks like thinking; it's actually
    over-caution.

    **Fix:** mid-call, tell your agent: *"turn off fast floor detection"* —
    the setting is read live, no restart. If the bot still feels timid, ask
    it to raise its barge-in grace.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Two bots answer every question in unison" icon="people-group">
    **What it means:** both bots heard the same prompt and neither could see
    the other had started speaking yet. They already draw randomized delays
    to break ties, but simultaneous starts still happen — calibration is
    ongoing.

    **Fix:** for a call with two bots, put one in **passive mode** (it then
    speaks only when addressed by name). Say: *"go passive unless we call
    on you."*
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="You asked for the whiteboard and nothing appeared at all" icon="chalkboard">
    **What it means:** a known bug, not you. The bot's update succeeds — the room
    state really does hold your content — but the shared render stays blank and
    shows "Whiteboard is empty." Two different bots reproduced it independently on
    0.8.13.

    **Since 0.8.14 the bot tells you.** A write that doesn't land now says so, and
    an unreadable board warns the agent while it can still talk — so it should say
    "the board isn't coming up" rather than insisting your content is on screen.
    If a bot still claims the board is up when it plainly isn't, that's worth
    reporting: it means the honesty check missed.

    **Fix for now:** ask for a **web page** instead — sharing a live browser tab
    works fine. "Pull up that page" does almost everything people want the
    whiteboard for, and it stays interactive.

    **Tell-tale:** the bot says the board is updated and the room sees nothing.
    If it's showing the *wrong* content rather than none, that's the different
    problem below.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The whiteboard isn't showing what the bot thinks it's showing" icon="chalkboard">
    **What it means:** each bot has its *own* whiteboard window. If another
    participant is presenting, your bot's update loads invisibly in its own
    window while the room keeps watching the presenter's share. Separately,
    content pushed right *before* a share starts can get re-rendered by the
    share itself.

    **Fix:** only the bot that is actually presenting can change what the
    room sees. If your bot IS presenting and the board looks stale, have it
    push the content again *after* the share started — and ask it to
    screenshot its own share to confirm what the room sees.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## When it's actually wedged — the recovery ladder

Most "broken" states above fix themselves without a restart, which is why every entry
says look first. But real wedges exist. Climb this ladder in order — each rung is less
destructive than the one below it:

1. **Look.** Hover the bot's name in the app panel — the tooltip shows the live local
   port. App running + port answering = not wedged; the problem is one of the entries
   above.
2. **Re-drive, don't restart.** A bot that seems stuck mid-call usually has a stalled
   session, not a stalled app: tell your Claude Code session to call `get_room_info`
   and continue. Never kill the app *during* a call — you'll strand a zombie tile in
   the Meet that the host has to remove.
3. **Rejoin.** If the bot's session is truly gone (session crashed, laptop slept),
   a fresh `/join-call` to the same Meet re-enters cleanly — leave first if the old
   tile is still in the room, then rejoin (leave → rejoin, never a second join on top).
4. **Quit and reopen the app** — the one known case that *requires* it: joins silently
   going nowhere **after a previous call ended** means the app is wedged
   ([#254](https://github.com/wanderingstan/vibeconf-app/issues/254)). Quit from the
   menu bar, reopen, `/join-call` again. Note the app may come back on a different
   port (it walks up from 7865) — see the first entry on this page.
